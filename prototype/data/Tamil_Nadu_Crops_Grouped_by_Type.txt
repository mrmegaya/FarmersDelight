Grouped by crop type
Prepared: October 2026

INTRODUCTION
Tamil Nadu has varied rainfall, soils and irrigation conditions. The main agricultural crop groups are paddy, millets and other cereals, pulses, oilseeds, cotton, sugarcane and horticultural crops. The list below covers crops commonly grown in the state, plus some crops cultivated only in suitable districts or specialised farms. It is a practical broad list, not a claim that every crop is grown widely in every district.

WHERE DIFFERENT GROUPS ARE COMMON
Cauvery Delta and irrigated delta areas: paddy, pulses and suitable rotation crops.
Western Tamil Nadu: coconut, maize, sorghum, turmeric, groundnut, cotton, tapioca, vegetables and fodder crops depending on district and water.
Krishnagiri and Dharmapuri belt: mango and other horticulture, along with millets, pulses and groundnut.
Nilgiris and cooler hill areas: tea and selected temperate vegetables, flowers and fruits.
Southern dryland districts: millets, pulses, cotton, chilli, sesame, groundnut and other drought-adapted crops.
Kanyakumari and other humid/suitable pockets: rubber, coconut, banana, spices and other tropical crops.
This is a regional guide only; crop suitability can differ between blocks and villages.
